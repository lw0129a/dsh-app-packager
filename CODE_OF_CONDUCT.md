# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- being kind and patient with questions, including questions about macOS/Windows differences this project keeps tripping over;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the project;
- showing empathy towards other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour: sexualised language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information without permission, and other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take appropriate, fair corrective action in response to any behaviour they deem inappropriate. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that are not aligned with this code of conduct.

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour through GitHub's private reporting on this repository (<https://github.com/lw0129a/dsh-app-packager/security/advisories/new>) or by contacting the maintainer [@lw0129a](https://github.com/lw0129a). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the reporter will be respected.
